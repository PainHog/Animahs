Ready-Made Bonded

Six Bonded from Wrenfield, one for each frame, ready to play tonight. Copy one onto a character sheet, or just play from this page. If two players want the same character, they each roll a die: the higher roll chooses first.

Maya Reyes, 12 · Jaguar

“If you can’t see me, I’m doing it right.”

Quiet, watchful, and the best hide-and-seek player Wrenfield Middle has ever had. Her jaguar found her on the wet roof of the garden centre on the night of the Waking. She loves the rooftop garden at the library.

Human: Sneak 3 · Force 1 · Move 2 · Sense 2 · Wits 1

Jaguar (Big Cats): Sneak 4 · Force 3 · Move 3 · Sense 2 · Strengths: Dark, High · Weaknesses: Water, Crowd

Night Rosettes (animal perk)

In Dark places, your Sneak rolls are at −1 Difficulty.

Hide-and-Seek Champ (human perk)

+1 die on Sneak rolls.

Mark: a fleck of gold in her left eye · Wild: 4
