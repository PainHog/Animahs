Chapter 3

Rolling the Dice

Most of the time you just say what your character does, and it happens. You roll only when it’s risky and could go either way: sneaking past a guard, leaping a gap, breaking open a cage. Every roll in the game works the same way.

The Roll

Roll as many d6 as your rating in the approach you’re using, in your current form, plus any bonus dice. Every 5 or 6 is a hit. The Difficulty, usually from 1 to 4, is the number of hits you need.

  • Fail: no hits at all.
  • Partial: at least one hit, but fewer than the Difficulty. You do it, but it costs you something.
  • Success: hits equal to or more than the Difficulty.
  • Critical: a Success with two or more 6s among the dice.

At Difficulty 1, a single hit is a Success, so there’s no Partial. If your rating in an approach is 0, you can’t roll it: an animal can’t roll Wits, for instance. The Storyteller says which approaches work for a problem and the Difficulty of each. Usually there’s more than one way.

Nobody rolls against you. The Storyteller never rolls dice. Guards, Hounds and locks just have a Difficulty, and what they do to you happens when you Fail or get a Partial.

The Cap

Bonus dice come from lots of places: Strengths, perks, friends’ Help and Edges. Some things lower the Difficulty instead. But they don’t pile up forever.

The Cap

From all sources together, one roll gets at most +2 dice and at most −1 Difficulty. Difficulty never goes below 1.

Things that make a roll harder, like a Weakness or a Hound’s Tracker, aren’t limited by the Cap, so a Difficulty can go above 4.

Wild

Wild is the power of your Bond, kept as tokens in front of you. You start each mission with 4. Spend 1 Wild to:

  • Shift into your animal (Chapter 4).
  • Push a roll you just made.
  • Help a friend’s roll.
  • Patch Up 1 Hurt (Chapter 7).

Unspent Wild is lost when the mission ends. Teams that hold their Wild back for later lose far more often than teams that spend it.

Push

Push

After you roll, you may spend 1 Wild to reroll every die that didn’t hit. Keep the new result. You can Push a roll only once. A Push isn’t a bonus die, so the Cap doesn’t limit it.

Help

Help

When a friend at the same obstacle is about to roll, you may spend 1 Wild to give them +1 die. Say how you help. Helping doesn’t use your action, so you can Help and still act yourself, before or after your own action. It has to be something your current form can do: in animal form you can’t help with Wits. Help counts towards the Cap (at most +2 dice from everything together), so if the roll already has a bonus die, only one friend’s Help adds anything.

Edges

An Edge is a lucky break the whole team shares. You gain Edges from a Critical when the extra progress isn’t needed (Chapter 5), from a good scouting roll (Chapter 5), and from the Team Captain perk. Spend an Edge for +1 die on anyone’s roll; it counts towards the Cap. The team holds at most 2 Edges at a time; any more are lost. Two Team Captains start the team with 2. Edges you haven’t spent are lost when the mission ends, like Wild.

Order of Things

When a roll happens, the result comes first, then its cost, then everything else. If a Partial both finishes a job and raises the Hunt’s Scent to its Limit, the job is still done before the Hunt comes running (Chapter 5).

Example: a Push and a Help

Jonah, as a bison, needs to break the bars of a cage: Force, Difficulty 3. Bison Force is 4. Heavies are strong against Locked things (+1 die), and his Football Player perk adds another (+1 die). Theo offers to Help, but Jonah is already at the Cap of +2 dice, so Theo keeps his Wild. Jonah rolls six dice and gets 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6: two hits, a Partial. He spends 1 Wild to Push and rerolls the four misses: 6, 2, 5, 1. That’s two more hits, four in all, with two 6s. A Critical!
