The night the lights went out, the Wild woke up.

You’re an ordinary kid in an ordinary town, and now you can become one real animal: a jaguar that vanishes in the dark, an owl that sees the whole yard, a bison that walks through doors. You didn’t ask for it. But someone in town has been waiting three hundred years for you.

The Gilded Hunt are rich, respectable and very patient, and they collect the Wild in gold. Now they’re planning the Last Hunt, to chain the Wild for good. You and your friends are all that stands in their way.

Second Nature is a roleplaying game for 3 to 5 players and a Storyteller, ages 10 and up. Inside: six animal frames and eighteen animals, simple d6 rules, five ready-to-play missions, six ready-made characters, and everything you need for your first night.

The Hunt wants what you are. Run wild.
