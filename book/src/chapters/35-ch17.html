Chapter 17 · Hard mission

The Lodge on the Hill

A Bonded boy is caged in the cellar of the Lodge on Thornhill, the Hunt’s own house. The team gets over the wall or under it, past the kennels and into the house, and breaks him out, along with any Taken friends of their own.

Read This Aloud

Thornhill looks different at night. The Lodge sits at the top like a crouching animal, with one lit window high under the roof. Somewhere below it, in a cellar, is Kit Marsh: twelve years old, Bonded to a brown hare, and Taken on Tuesday walking home from school. In three nights the moon will be full, and the Hunt will make him into a Trophy. Not if you get there first.

What’s Going On

The Lodge is Lady Cordelia Vane’s house and the Hunt’s home. It’s heavily guarded: two Hounds in the deer park, three in the kennels, two Trappers in the cellar, and the Masters asleep upstairs.

Rescuing a friend: if one of your team was Taken on an earlier mission, they’re in the cellar cage too. Free the cage and you free them both. A rescued character rejoins the team when you’re out, but doesn’t roll during the Escape.

The Places

Way in A: The Deer Park

A long, dark, open slope of grass and old oaks between the wall and the house, with two Hounds loose on it. Everyone has to cross. If anyone Fails, the Hounds come.

Way in B: The Ice-House Tunnel

An old brick tunnel runs from a forgotten ice house in Wrenwood up under the lawn, half flooded and very narrow. It’s cold and the water is deep in places: a Fail deals 1 Hurt.

Inside 1: The Kennels

The way to the house runs past the kennels: a narrow, dark yard with three Hounds behind half-doors. Everyone has to get past without waking them. A patient watcher can learn their rhythm (Sense 3). If anyone Fails, it’s a Clash.

Inside 2: The Servants’ Door

The back door of the house, locked and bolted. Clever hands or a heavy shoulder.

Inside 3: The Trophy Hall

A long, dark hall hung with the Hunt’s old Trophies in glass frames, with a gallery above. Somewhere in here is the way down to the cellar, behind a panel nobody is meant to find. Search, sniff, look.

The Objective: The Cellar Cage

A narrow stone cellar with a gilded cage, and two Trappers playing cards at a table by the stairs. The cage is locked with three old locks. If anyone Fails while the Trappers are there, it’s a Clash, and beating them doesn’t open the cage: you still need 3 progress. When it opens, Kit (and any Taken friend of yours) comes with you. Kit is too weak to shift; he goes where you go, and doesn’t roll.

Escape 1: The Lawn

Out through the garden door and across the wide front lawn, in full view of the house.

Escape 2: The Wall

The old wall around the grounds: climb it, fly it, or break through the rotten gate.

Threats

Two Hounds in the deer park, three Hounds in the kennels, and two Trappers in the cellar (Chapter 10). If the Hunt is Up, the Masters wake, and the lit window under the roof goes dark.

Spotlights

Critters and Reptiles shine in the ice-house tunnel and the kennels; Canines and Heavies in the deer park and on the lawn; Birds and Big Cats in the trophy hall and on the wall. The servants’ door and the cellar locks want hands, or a Heavy.

After the Mission

  • Win: Kit is home by dawn, and his hare is back in Wrenwood. The Hunt has been beaten in its own house, and it won’t forget it.
  • Costly Win: Kit is free, but one of the team now sits in the cage he left. The full moon is still coming.
  • Loss: the cellar stays locked. If nobody tries again before the full moon, Kit’s Bond becomes a Trophy (Chapter 8).
