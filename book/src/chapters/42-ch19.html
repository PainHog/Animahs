Chapter 19

At the Table

The whole game on one page, for the middle of the table. Every rule here is explained in full in the chapter given in brackets.

The Roll (Ch. 3)

Roll your rating in the approach (your current form), plus bonus dice. 5 or 6 = hit. Difficulty = hits needed. Fail: no hits. Partial: some hits, fewer than the Difficulty. Success: enough hits. Critical: a Success with two or more 6s. The Cap: at most +2 dice and −1 Difficulty per roll, from all sources. Threats never roll.

Wild (Ch. 3, 4, 7)

Start each mission with 4 (5 for a Gamer). Spend 1 to: Shift into your animal (your action); Push (reroll every miss, once per roll, not limited by the Cap); Help a friend’s roll (+1 die, no action; your form must be able to do it); Patch Up 1 Hurt (your action). Unspent Wild is lost. Edges (team, at most 2): +1 die on any roll.

Shifting (Ch. 4)

Into your animal: 1 Wild (or 1 Hurt if you have none) and your action. Back: free, your action. No time limit. Between obstacles, everyone may Shift or Patch Up once for free; that’s out of sight. A shift in a Clash is in sight: you’re Exposed. Animals can’t talk, read, use tools or roll Wits; they carry one small thing. Strength in a place’s tags: +1 die. Weakness: +1 Difficulty.

Obstacles (Ch. 5)

Point: one roll per round, by anyone; progress adds up. Group: everyone not yet past rolls; Partial or better gets you past. Success: 1 progress. Critical: +1 extra progress, or an Edge. Partial: 1 progress, Scent +1. Fail: no progress, Scent +1; dangerous: 1 Hurt; guarded: a Clash. A group obstacle raises Scent once, when it ends (+1 for any Partial or Fail), and +1 for every round after the third. In the Escape, Partials cost no Scent.

Clashes (Ch. 6)

Each round, everyone acts once: Strike (Force; Success 1 harm, Critical 2), Distract (Sneak or Move; next Strike on it this round −1 Difficulty), Slip Away (Move or Sneak; you’re out and past), Shift, Patch Up, or free a Netted friend. Partial: it works, but you take the threat’s Harm. Fail: you take its Harm. Each threat nobody rolled against hurts someone. Ends when all threats are out, nobody’s left, or after round 4. Then Scent +1, or +2 if it reached round 4, someone went Down, or someone shifted.

Scent and the Hunt (Ch. 5)

Scent never goes down. On the Trail (half the Limit or more, rounded up, as an Escape obstacle begins): that obstacle is +1 Difficulty. The Hunt is Up (at the Limit): any Fail means you’re Taken; keep going or Withdraw (a Loss).

Hurt and Worse (Ch. 7)

3 Hurt boxes; Hurt never lowers your dice. Down at 3: out until the scene ends, then back up with 2 Hurt if a friend stands and the Hunt isn’t Up; otherwise Taken. Down as an animal: Stuck. Netted (a Trapper on your Fail): a friend frees you, or you’re freed at scene end on the same terms. Taken: out for the mission; the prize is lost with you. Exposed: next mission starts at Scent 1. Everyone down at once: everyone Taken.

A Mission (Ch. 5)

Scout (Sense 2: Critical = pick the way in, 2 Edges; Success = pick, 1 Edge; Partial = pick, Scent 1; Fail = Storyteller picks, Scent 1) → way in → obstacles → objective → Escape. Win: done, nobody Taken. Costly Win: done, someone Taken. Loss: not done.
