Chapter 4

Shifting

It feels like jumping into cold water and coming up as somebody else, except the somebody else is still you.

When you shift, the Wild wraps around you in a swirl of light and leaves, and a moment later the animal steps out. Nobody sees the in-between, not even you. One second there’s a kid; the next there’s a barn owl blinking on the fence post, wearing that kid’s freckles.

Shifting

  • Shifting into your animal costs 1 Wild and takes your action.
  • Out of Wild? You can still shift into your animal by taking 1 Hurt instead, unless that would put you Down.
  • Shifting back to human costs nothing, but it also takes your action.
  • There’s no time limit. Stay an animal all night if you like.

When You Can Shift

Shifting takes your action, so you can shift instead of rolling in a round (Chapter 5). And as you move from one obstacle to the next, everyone may Shift or Patch Up once without it using an action. Most shifts happen there, in the gaps between obstacles.

Being Seen

In Sight of the Hunt

Between obstacles you can always find cover, so a shift there is out of sight, unless the mission says you’re being watched. So is a shift you take as your action at an obstacle. A shift during a Clash is always in sight. If the Hunt sees you shift, its Scent rises by 1 (in a Clash, the Clash’s own rise covers it: Chapter 6), and you’re Exposed (Chapter 7).

What Comes With You

  • Your Hurt comes with you. A bruised kid is a bruised fox.
  • Your Wild is yours in either form.
  • Your human perk works in both forms, unless it says human form. You’re still you in there.
  • Your clothes and small personal things (phone, glasses, keys) vanish into the Wild and come back when you do.
  • Anything else you’re holding drops at your feet when you shift. That includes the prize you came to steal.

Being an Animal

In animal form you use your frame’s ratings and your Strengths and Weaknesses (Chapter 2). You’re the real animal at its real size, with its real senses. You also lose some things:

  • You can’t speak, read, or use hands or tools. Your Wits rating is 0, so you can’t roll Wits, or Help a friend’s Wits roll. (The Raven’s Clever Beak lets a Raven roll Wits against Locked things, but even a Raven can’t Help with Wits.)
  • You can carry one small thing in your jaws, beak or claws: a key, a Trophy, a note.
  • You can’t talk to your friends. Bonded don’t share thoughts. Agree on simple signals before a mission: one bark means danger, a tap on the glass means “here”. At the table, of course, the players can talk as much as they like.

Human or Animal?

Shifting is never free, and staying an animal isn’t always best. Before each obstacle, look at what you’ll need:

  • Locked doors and people to talk to are easiest with Wits, and Wits needs human form. (A locked door can also be broken with Force.)
  • Crowds are a Weakness for Big Cats, Heavies and Reptiles. A kid in a crowd is invisible; a jaguar is not.
  • Your Weaknesses can make a human roll the better bet. A cat at the moat might do better swimming as a kid.
  • Everything physical, like sneaking, climbing, fighting and tracking, is usually better as an animal.

A good team spreads out: one or two stay human for the doors and the talking while the others go wild.

Stuck

If you go Down while in animal form (Chapter 7), the Wild holds on to you: you’re Stuck as your animal until the mission ends. You can’t shift back until then. Getting a moose home through town without being seen makes a fine scene for the end of the night.

Example: Choosing Forms

The team reaches the loading bay of the museum: a point obstacle, Dark and Locked, needing 2 progress, with Sneak 3, Force 3 or Wits 2. Maya is already a jaguar: Sneak 4, +1 die for the Dark and +1 for her Hide-and-Seek Champ perk, and Night Rosettes brings the Difficulty down to 2. Six dice against 2 is the team’s best roll, so she slips under the shutter to work it from inside. Priya stays human for the second progress: Wits 3 and her Brainiac perk, four dice against 2. Jonah shifts into his bison on the way, which costs him 1 Wild but no action: Force 4, +1 die for Locked and +1 for his Football Player perk is six dice against 3, ready if the others miss. As a bison he can’t Help Priya’s Wits roll.

Designer’s Note

There’s no countdown on your animal form, and nothing bad happens if you stay too long. The cost of the Wild is paid going in: a token, an action, and everything an animal can’t do. That keeps the choice simple at the table: “Is it worth a Wild to be a bear for this?”
