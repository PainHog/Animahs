Chapter 6

Clashes

Sometimes sneaking fails, and sometimes a wall of grey Hounds just won’t let you by. A Clash is a fight: quick, loud, and over in a few rounds. Clashes are noisy, so the Hunt always hears them.

Threats

Everyone you fight is a threat with three numbers:

  • Difficulty: the hits you need to land a blow on it, slip it, or distract it.
  • Toughness: how much harm it takes before it’s out (knocked down, chased off or tied up).
  • Harm: how much Hurt it deals to you.

Some threats also have a special rule. All of them are in Chapter 10. Threats never roll; they hurt you when you slip up.

A Round of a Clash

Each round, everyone in the Clash takes one action, in any order the players like:

Clash Actions

  • Strike (Force) a threat. Success: 1 harm. Critical: 2 harm. When a threat has taken harm equal to its Toughness, it’s out.
  • Distract (Sneak or Move) a threat. A Success puts the next Strike against it this round at −1 Difficulty.
  • Slip Away (Move or Sneak) from a threat. A Success takes you out of the Clash. At a group obstacle you’re past it; at a point obstacle you wait, out of harm’s way, until the Clash ends.
  • Shift, Patch Up, or free a Netted friend (no roll).

Against a threat, a Partial does the job, but the threat’s Harm hits you. A Fail just hits you.

At the end of each round, each threat that nobody rolled against deals its Harm to one character in the Clash, chosen by the Storyteller.

You can Help a friend’s roll in a Clash just as anywhere else: 1 Wild for +1 die, without using your action. Edges work too.

That last rule matters. Leave a Hound alone and it bites someone. Every threat needs somebody rolling against it, even if that’s just a Critter Distracting it so the bison can Strike.

How a Clash Ends

The End of a Clash

A Clash ends when every threat is out, when nobody is left in it, or at the end of round 4. Then any threats still standing pull back to wait for the Masters.

When a Clash ends, Scent rises once: +1, or +2 if it reached round 4, someone went Down, or someone shifted during it. Anyone who shifted during it is Exposed.

After a Clash, Down and Netted friends get back up if anyone is still standing and the Hunt isn’t Up (Chapter 7). If the Clash started at a guarded obstacle, beating the guards (or seeing them pull back) clears it, unless the obstacle still needs progress.

Fighting Smart

  • Shift before the fight, not during it. If an obstacle is guarded, decide on the way there who’s going to be the bison. Shifting mid-Clash costs your action, raises the Clash’s Scent to +2, and Exposes you.
  • Pick on one threat at a time. A Hound with 1 Toughness left is still biting.
  • Small animals Distract. A mouse can’t Strike for much, but a Distract on a Hound’s nose makes the big friend’s Strike much easier.
  • Get it done in three rounds. Round 4 costs an extra Scent.
  • Sometimes don’t fight at all. Slip Away is a fine choice when the objective is somewhere else.

Example: Two Hounds on the Lawn

The Hound patrol crosses a dark, open lawn (Open, Dark). Lucía Fails to sneak past, and two Hounds turn: Difficulty 2, Toughness 3, Harm 1. The team was ready: Jonah and Theo shifted on the way in.

Round 1. Priya the barn owl Distracts the first Hound by swooping at its face. She rolls Move: Bird Move 4, +1 die for the Open lawn (a Bird Strength) and +1 die for Silent Wings in the Dark, six dice against Difficulty 2. A Success. Jonah the bison Strikes that Hound: Force 4, +1 die for the Open lawn and +1 for his Football Player perk, against Difficulty 1 thanks to the Distract. A Success: 1 harm. Theo the coyote Strikes the second Hound with four dice (Force 3, +1 for the Open lawn) against Difficulty 2 and gets one hit, a Partial: 1 harm, and the Hound bites back for 1 Hurt. Lucía the alligator Strikes the first Hound with four dice (Force 3, +1 for the Dark, a Reptile Strength): a Success. Both Hounds were rolled against, so neither attacks at the end of the round.

Round 2. The first Hound has 1 Toughness left, the second 2. Jonah Strikes the second and rolls a Critical: 2 harm, and it’s out. Lucía Strikes the first and Succeeds: it’s out too.

The Clash ends in round 2. Nobody went Down and nobody shifted during it, so it raises Scent by 1. Beating the guards clears the lawn, and as the obstacle ends, Lucía’s Fail raises Scent by 1 more.
