Chapter 16 · Hard mission

The Trophy Gala

Once a year the Ashcombe Museum throws a gala for its patrons, and this year the Hunt is showing off its newest Trophy: a silver pendant holding the Bond of a girl from the next town, whose animal was an otter. The team gets into a crowded party, through the dark halls where the Hounds wait, steals the pendant from its guarded case and carries it out.

Read This Aloud

The museum’s windows blaze with light, and a string quartet is playing in the ballroom. Everyone who matters in Wrenfield is here, in their best clothes. In a glass case by the dinosaur, under a little card that says “Historic Hunting Jewellery, on loan from a private collection”, lies a pendant shaped like a drop of water. Somewhere a girl named Isla Brook is sitting at home, unable to remember what it felt like to swim like an otter. You’re going to bring it back to her.

What’s Going On

The gala is how the Hunt shows off to its members and tempts new ones. Most guests are ordinary townsfolk who have no idea what the jewellery case really holds. A Huntsman stands by the case all evening, smiling at everyone. Two Hounds and a Trapper wait in the dark halls between the ballroom and the case. If the pendant leaves the building, its Bond can go home to Isla.

The Places

Way in A: The Front Doors

Guests arrive in a crowd at the front doors, past a man checking invitations. Kids in human form can talk their way in as somebody’s children; an animal will cause a commotion.

Way in B: The Loading Bay

Round the side, in the dark, the caterers’ loading bay has a roller shutter and a locked door into the kitchens behind the ballroom. One of you has to get it open.

Inside 1: The Ballroom

Chandeliers, a quartet, waiters with trays, and two hundred guests. Everyone has to cross it without being stopped. Blend in, or slip along the walls.

Inside 2: The Dark Exhibit Halls

Beyond the ballroom the museum is unlit: tall cases of stuffed animals, a mezzanine above, and two Hounds and a Trapper on watch. Everyone has to get through. If anyone Fails, it’s a Clash.

The Objective: The Trophy Case

The glass case stands under a spotlight in the Hall of Hunters, at the far end of the dark halls, beside the dinosaur. All evening the Huntsman beside it brings small groups of guests to admire it, so there’s always a crowd. The lock is tricky and the glass is thick. If anyone Fails while the Huntsman is there, it’s a Clash, and beating him doesn’t open the case. When it opens, someone takes the pendant: it’s a prize to carry out, small enough for jaws or a pocket. If its carrier is Taken, it’s lost.

Escape 1: The Galleries

Back through the dark halls and along the upper galleries, with the gala starting to notice that something is wrong.

Escape 2: The Museum Steps

Out through a side door and down the wide front steps, into the open, past guests waiting for their cars.

Threats

Two Hounds and a Trapper in the halls, and a Huntsman at the case (Chapter 10). If the Hunt is Up, Lady Cordelia Vane herself rises from her seat at the gala, and the doors begin to close.

Spotlights

This is the Crowd mission. Canines and Critters blend in where Big Cats, Heavies and Reptiles can’t, and kids in human form are at their best at the front doors and in the ballroom. Big Cats and Birds rule the dark, high halls. Plan who shifts when; there isn’t much cover in a ballroom.

After the Mission

  • Win: the pendant reaches Isla, and the next morning she’s in the river, laughing. The Hunt knows the Bonded were at the gala.
  • Costly Win: Isla gets her Bond back, but one of the team doesn’t come home.
  • Loss: the pendant goes back to the Lodge. Isla’s Bond stays chained in gold until someone frees it.
