Chapter 8

Campaigns

One mission makes a great evening. A campaign tells the whole story: six missions against the Gilded Hunt, each one bringing you closer to the seventh, the night of the Last Hunt, when the Masters will try to chain the Old Wild for good.

The Hunt Track

The Hunt Track

The campaign has one Hunt track, from 0 to 6, on the mission tracker. It starts at 0.

  • After a Win, it doesn’t move.
  • After a Costly Win, it rises by 1.
  • After a Loss, it rises by 2.

When it reaches 6, the Last Hunt comes early: the next mission is the Last Hunt. Otherwise the Last Hunt is the seventh mission. The track never goes down.

Growth

Growth

After every mission, whatever the result, every character marks 1 Growth. When you reach 2, 4 and 6 Growth, choose one:

  • +1 Wild: start every mission with 1 more Wild, to a maximum of 6, counting the Gamer perk.
  • A second human perk (a different one).
  • +1 to a human rating, to a maximum of 3.
  • Wild Sense: choose one of your frame’s Strengths. It now works in human form too.

You can’t choose the same thing twice, except +1 to a human rating (for a different rating each time).

Your Bond grows; your animal doesn’t change. You keep one animal for life.

Exposed

If anyone ended a mission Exposed, the Hunt is watching the team. The next mission starts at Scent 1 (only 1, however many of you were Exposed). After that mission, Exposed clears.

Taken Friends

A Taken character is held by the Hunt. The Hunt means to bind their Bond into a new Trophy on the next full moon: in a campaign, the rescue can come on either of the next two missions, and after that it’s too late. Rescuing them can be the team’s next mission (The Lodge on the Hill, Chapter 17, is written as a rescue), or a Storyteller can add the rescue to another mission as one more point obstacle before the Escape: a locked cage, Force 3 or Wits 2, 3 progress, guarded or not. A Taken character marks Growth after the mission like everyone else.

While a character is Taken, their player makes a new Bonded (or takes a ready-made one) who joins at the start of the next mission, never during one. A new character starts with Growth equal to the lowest in the group. When a Taken friend is rescued, their player chooses which of their two characters to play from then on; the other becomes a friend of the team.

If a Taken friend isn’t rescued before the full moon, the Hunt makes their Trophy. They lose their Bond and become a normal kid again, but they remember everything. Freeing that Trophy from the Hunt gives them their Bond back; the Storyteller can make that a mission of its own.

Everyone Taken

If the whole team is Taken, play a Breakout next: every player makes a new Bonded (or takes a ready-made one), and the new team runs The Lodge on the Hill to free the old one. The new characters start with the old team’s lowest Growth. The Breakout comes first even if the Hunt track has just reached 6: the Last Hunt follows it.

The Last Hunt

The Last Hunt is the campaign’s final mission, on the night the Masters gather to chain the Old Wild. Build it as a Hard mission (Chapter 12) at the place the campaign has been leading to. The higher the Hunt track, the harder it gets: if the Hunt track is 4 or more, lower the mission’s Limit by 1. Win it, or win it at a cost, and the Old Wild stays free, the Trophies break, and every Bonded animal the Hunt ever took runs home (a friend Taken that night is freed with them). Lose it, and the story isn’t over, but the world has become a much tamer place.

Designer’s Note

The campaign rules in this chapter haven’t been simulated yet; the five missions in Part Three have. Expect Growth, the Hunt track and the Last Hunt to change after playtesting. If something feels off at your table, tell us.

Between Missions

Between missions, you’re kids again: school, homework, families who wonder why you’re so tired. You don’t need rules for this. A few minutes of play between missions, such as spotting a Huntsman at the school gate or hearing a rumour at the shops, is a good way to lead into the next brief.
