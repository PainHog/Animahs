Chapter 11

Wrenfield

Wrenfield is a small river town with one high street, one cinema, two schools and a hill nobody climbs. You can play in your own town instead (it’s more fun when you know the streets), but Wrenfield is where the missions in this book take place, and it’s ready to use.

The Night of the Waking

Last October a storm knocked out every light in Wrenfield for one hour. In that hour the zoo’s fences failed, animals got loose all over town, and six children each found themselves face to face with one of them. By morning the animals were back in their pens or gone into the woods, and the children were Bonded. Most people remember it as “the night the zoo got out”. The Hunt remembers it very differently.

Places

1. Pemberton’s Pets

A cramped old pet shop at the end of the High Street. Old Mrs Pemberton ran it for forty years and sold it last spring to a polite company nobody had heard of. The new manager, Mr Rook, keeps odd hours, and the strays of Wrenfield have started to disappear. Mission: Chapter 13.

2. Wrenfield Zoo

A small, much-loved zoo by the river, with a moat, an aviary and a famous pair of snow leopards. Since the night of the Waking, its director, Dr Imogen Hale, has hired new “transport staff” who work only at night. Mission: Chapter 14.

3. The Old Mill

A tall brick mill on the River Wren, with a working waterwheel the town keeps for show. Its owner, Mr Corwin Thatch, has been building something in the tower. The Hounds have been restless ever since. Mission: Chapter 15.

4. The Ashcombe Museum

Wrenfield’s natural history museum: dark halls of stuffed animals, a dinosaur skeleton, and a glass case of “historic hunting jewellery” that is never quite the same twice. Once a year it hosts a grand gala for its patrons. Mission: Chapter 16.

5. The Lodge on Thornhill

A great stone house on the wooded hill above the town, behind a deer park and a high wall. It belongs to Lady Cordelia Vane, Master of the Hunt in Wrenfield, though in town she’s known only as a generous patron of the museum and the zoo. Mission: Chapter 17.

Elsewhere in Town

  • The High Street: the chip shop, the bakery, the cinema, and the bus stop where everyone meets.
  • Wrenfield Library: warm, quiet, and full of old maps. The librarian, Ms Dara Quill, has been reading about the Hunt for years and is the closest thing the Bonded have to an adult ally.
  • Wrenwood: the woods between the town and Thornhill, full of badgers, owls and old paths. A good place to shift unseen.
  • The River Wren: slow and brown, with a towpath, a weir by the mill, and drains that run under half the town.
  • Wrenfield Middle School: where most of the Bonded spend their days, trying to stay awake.
