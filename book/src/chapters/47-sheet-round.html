Storyteller’s Round Card

Keep this in front of you. You never roll; you describe, set Difficulties and keep the Scent track.

An obstacle
1Describe it and its tags. Say which approaches work and their Difficulties, and whether it’s point or group, guarded or dangerous.
2Point: one player rolls this round; others may Help. Group: everyone not yet past rolls.
3Resolve: Success = progress; Critical = +1 progress or an Edge; Partial = progress and Scent +1 (point obstacles); Fail = no progress and Scent +1, plus Hurt if dangerous, plus a Clash if guarded (or Taken if the Hunt is Up).
4Group obstacle ending? Scent +1 if anyone got a Partial or Fail (only Fails in the Escape). Still going after round 3? Scent +1 every round.
5Scene over: Down and Netted friends get up (2 Hurt) if anyone is standing and the Hunt isn’t Up; otherwise they’re Taken. Once-per-scene abilities come back.
A Clash round
1Everyone takes one action, in any order they like: Strike, Distract, Slip Away, Shift, Patch Up, free a friend. Help can come at any time.
2Resolve each roll as it’s made. Partial: it works, and the threat’s Harm hits them. Fail: Harm only (a Trapper nets instead).
3Each threat nobody rolled against hurts one character of your choice.
4Ended? (All threats out, nobody left, or round 4 done.) Then Scent +1, or +2 if it reached round 4, someone went Down, or someone shifted (they’re Exposed).
