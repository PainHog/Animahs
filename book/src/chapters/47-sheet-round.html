Storyteller’s Round Card

Keep this in front of you. You never roll; you describe, set Difficulties and keep the Scent track.

An obstacle
1Describe it and its tags. Say which approaches work and their Difficulties, and whether it’s point or group, guarded or dangerous.
2Point: one player rolls this round; others may Help. Group: everyone not yet past rolls.
3Resolve: Success = 1 progress. Critical = 2 progress (1 and an Edge if the extra isn’t needed). Partial = 1 progress. Fail = no progress, 1 Hurt if dangerous, a Clash if guarded. At a point obstacle, a Partial or a Fail is Scent +1. Once the Hunt is Up, any Fail means Taken.
4Group obstacle ending, or a guarded Fail’s Clash about to start? Scent +1 if anyone got a Partial or Fail since the last rise (only Fails in the Escape). From round 3, anyone not past at a round’s end: Scent +1.
5Scene over: if anyone is standing and the Hunt isn’t Up, Down friends get up with 2 Hurt and Netted friends are freed; otherwise they’re Taken. Once-per-scene abilities come back.
A Clash round
1Everyone takes one action, in any order they like: Strike, Distract, Slip Away, Shift, Patch Up, free a friend. Help can come at any time.
2Resolve each roll as it’s made. Partial: it works, and the threat’s Harm hits them. Fail: Harm only (a Trapper nets instead).
3Each threat nobody rolled against hurts one character of your choice.
4Ended? (All threats out; round 4 done; or nobody who can act left, and then the guards stay.) Then Scent +1, or +2 if it reached round 4, someone went Down, or someone shifted (they’re Exposed).
